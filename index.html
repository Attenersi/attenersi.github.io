A multi-page Power BI report on nuclear adoption and carbon intensity across European grids. The headline finding: a −0.40 correlation between nuclear share and grams of CO₂ per kWh. Not a causation claim, but a pattern that holds up across countries.

.03
Operations Research · Client Project

DKG Logistics Optimisation

Client project for DKG Keukens. The problem: how to load mixed cargo of heavy and fragile kitchen units into one truck without the bottom layer crushing the top. We tried ML first. It kept producing mathematically tidy answers that ignored physics. So we switched to a heuristic algorithm and built a 3D packing visualisation around it. Plus a safety framework, because warehouse staff need to trust the output before they'll use it.
